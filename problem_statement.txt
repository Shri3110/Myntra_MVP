[PROBLEM CONTEXT]
High-intent fashion shoppers on Myntra abandon wishlisted items because the current 
wishlist serves as a passive storage folder rather than an active decision-support tool[cite: 1, 2]. 
Lacking real-world fit, fabric, and styling validation within the app, users migrate 
off-platform to research on social media—leaving items to sit until they go out of stock[cite: 1, 2].

[MVP CORE PROBLEM STATEMENT]
High-intent fashion shoppers abandon wishlisted items due to pre-purchase decision 
uncertainty (fit, sizing, real-body context) and silent inventory depletion, resulting in 
a low 30-day wishlist-to-cart conversion rate[cite: 1, 2].

[ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS (5 WHYS)]
Problem: Low 30-day wishlist-to-purchase conversion rate.
├── Why? -> Users leave items sitting in their wishlist without moving them to the cart[cite: 1, 2].
│     │
│     ├── Why? -> Users lack decision confidence (sizing, fit, and styling) while viewing saved items[cite: 1, 2].
│     │     │
│     │     └── Why? -> PDPs rely on studio models, forcing users to leave Myntra for Instagram/YouTube for real-body try-on content[cite: 1, 2].
│     │
│     └── Why? -> Saved items go out of stock in specific sizes before users finish evaluating them[cite: 1, 2].
│           │
│           └── Why? -> The wishlist lacks dynamic inventory tracking and proactive size-depletion alerts[cite: 1, 2].
